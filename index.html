About Frontier

A place to learn practical skills.

Frontier Typing Short Hand College is dedicated to skill development through focused training and regular practice. The institute offers three main courses: Typing, Shorthand and DIT.

Our aim is to help students improve their speed, accuracy, computer knowledge and confidence, giving them useful skills for their next step in education and career development.
